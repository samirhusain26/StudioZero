## Problems found

- **Enclave hidden on Tectonic maps.** The globe data has no Lesotho hole in South Africa, so a host drawn after an enclave covers it. Found in tectonic-missing-003 (Botswana; South Africa is a neighbor). MissingCountry was fixed before approval (`enclosedBy` in `studio/src/episodes/templates/tectonic/geoKit.ts`; decision B09) and only tectonic-missing-003 was re-rendered (`review/jobs/tectonic-missing-003/status.json`: two attempts, 01:38Z and 01:57Z, different `codeHash`).
- **NeighborClues lacked the same redraw.** None of this batch's neighbor-clues episodes (001 Mexico, 002 Gabon, 003 Malawi) shows an enclave, so they were unaffected. Fixed on 2026-10-08 after finalization (rebuild Phase 6): enclaves inside the target, a neighbor or a context country are redrawn above their host, label anchors and card silhouettes treat them as holes, and an enclave's outline counts as its shared border. Stills of all three b01 neighbor-clues episodes are byte-identical before and after the fix. The kit `codeHash` changed (`render.mjs` reports earlier jobs as `staleKit`); `render.mjs` refuses to run on a finalized batch, so b01's files are untouched.
- **Neighbor label crowding** at the reveal (frame 570, 19 s): tectonic-neighbors-003 "Malawi" and "Mozambique" touch; tectonic-neighbors-002 "Equatorial Guinea" and "Republic of the Congo" stack tightly over Cameroon with long leader lines. Not fixed.
- **Elimination clue 3 removed no choices** in all three candidate-elimination episodes: `evidence.checks.eliminatedPerClue` is `[["CHL"],["BRA"],[]]` (001), `[["KGZ"],["KAZ"],[]]` (002) and `[["IDN"],["VNM"],[]]` (003). The third clue only confirms the answer, so the elimination format loses its last beat. Not fixed.
- **The Uzbekistan explanation doesn't tie to the clues** (scalar-elimination-002): clues are 5 neighbors, under 500,000 km², over 10 million people; the explanation is about Uzbekistan and all its neighbors being landlocked. The fact-check flagged it too ("does not restate the clue values", `library/2026-10-pilots/review/source-verification.json`, key `scalar-elim-uzb`). Not fixed.
- **Guess-the-year: a choice marker scrolls off-screen** when the answer is A or C. whenabouts-year-002 (answer A 1215): after the reveal the timeline centres on 1215 and the C marker (1415) is off the right edge. whenabouts-year-003 (answer C 1687): the A marker (1567) is off the left edge. The pilot whenabouts-year-001 (answer B) keeps all three. Full-resolution stills at 14 s and 15.5 s show it. Not fixed.

## Process lessons

- `review.mjs` renders review frames at half scale (`--scale=0.5`). Use full-resolution stills for maps: borders, enclaves and small labels are lost at half scale.
- Remotion's bundled ffmpeg has no `tile` filter (`remotion ffmpeg -filters` lists no `tile`). Build contact sheets with PIL: `studio/.venv/bin/python studio/render/contact.py out.png a.png b.png …` (it draws old red zones; ignore them).
