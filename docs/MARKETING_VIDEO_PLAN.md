## Goal

Drive first plays to scalargame.com from Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts. Success is a viewer who taps through and completes one puzzle, not a viewer who watches all four games.

Why one puzzle: the July PostHog audit found ~82% of visitors play once and never return. The call to action is therefore "play one, it takes 3 minutes." The four-game set and the streak are the second message.

## Creative principles

1. **Hook inside 1 second.** A slam, a question or a riddle, never a logo.
2. **Everything lands on the beat.** All music is 120 BPM: 1 beat = 0.5s, 1 bar = 2s. Every cut, slam and reveal is quantized to the beat grid.
3. **Loop it.** Last frame flows into the first frame. Loops raise watch-time and replays.
4. **Open loops keep people watching.** The hero opens with a riddle and only answers it at the end.
5. **Real product, real data.** Only use real game data, real recorded runs and the real numbers below. No invented player counts, testimonials or scores.
6. **Fun over polish.** Overshoot springs, stamps, sticker pops, screen shake. It should feel like a game.

## Visual and motion language ("Scalar Pop")

Built on the site's own system, turned up.

- **Paper world:** `#FAFAF9` paper, `#18181B` charcoal, sharp corners, 1px borders, hard-offset shadows (6px) that "press flat" on impact. Fraunces for giant headlines; Geist Mono for captions and data. Light paper grain.
- **Thermal feedback as fireworks:** green `#22C55E` exact, orange `#F97316` hot, amber-dashed near, white miss. Cells flip in cascades. Glyphs ✓ ● ◐ ○ rain in as confetti.
- **Per-game accents** flood the screen on transitions: Scalar teal `#14B8A6`, Continuum pink `#F472B6`, Whenabouts yellow `#EAB308`, Tectonic orange `#F2A154`.
- **Transition motif:** the Scalar **Venn circles** overlap-wipe between games. The one non-Venn transition is the **paper-to-dark "power off"** cut into Tectonic, timed to the beat drop.
- **Motion rules:** hard cuts on beat, no crossfades; punch-in to ~105% on strong beats; spring overshoot on entrances; rotated stamps (about −6°) for results; rolling counters; screen shake only on wrong answers.
- **Safe zones (1080×1920):** keep key content clear of the top ~14%, the bottom ~20% and the right ~12% rail, where Reels and Shorts overlay their UI. Captions sit around y 1150–1400.

## Audio plan

- **Music:** upbeat, quirky, playful, 120 BPM, generated by the owner (see [Music delivery spec](#music-delivery-spec)). It needs a clear **build** into a **drop on a bar line**, which the hero cuts to for Tectonic.
- **Tempo tolerance:** AI tools drift. Anything 116–124 BPM is usable; the build session measures the real BPM with ffmpeg, time-stretches within ±5% if needed, and sets `BPM` in `src/theme.ts` to match. Section lengths can be re-cut on bar lines to land the drop exactly.
- **Sound design (we author these):** key ticks; flip whooshes on each cell; a satisfying *thock* on every lock-in; a rubbery shake sting on wrong; a rewind whoosh for the Whenabouts scrubber; a stamp thud on results; a coin-style ding on PAS counters.
- **Tectonic ASMR:** no music track. Organic sound only: soft ocean bed plus each placement as a pentatonic note, so the globe "plays" itself. Placements land on the beat grid so the visuals and notes lock together.
- **Licensing:** confirm the AI music tool's plan grants **commercial-use** rights for the tracks you generate (many free tiers don't). Keep the plan/terms noted in `public/music/TRACKS.md`. Check each platform's current rules on AI-generated audio before posting.

### Music delivery spec

The owner generates the tracks with prompts kept outside this doc. Requirements the tracks must meet:

- **Length:** hero tracks 38–45s, teaser and play-along tracks 15s. Anything longer is trimmed on bar lines in the build session, so only the first 45s matter.
- **Tempo:** 120 BPM (116–124 usable), instrumental, no tempo changes.
- **Hero structure (1 bar = 2s):** 2-bar riff intro, build through bar 11, **drop on bar 12 (0:22)**, full energy to bar 15, breakdown or hit at bars 16–17, resolving ending at bars 18–19.
- **Files:** `~/Personal/code_projects/scalar-video/public/music/`, named like `hero-a-01.mp3`, one row each in `TRACKS.md` (tool, plan, prompt). BPM and drop time are optional; the build session measures them.
- **Licensing:** the AI tool's plan must grant commercial-use rights.

## Hero script: "Four ways to know the world" (38s, 19 bars at 120 BPM)

All timings are bar-aligned (1 bar = 2s) so cuts and the music drop land together.

| Bars | Time | Scene | On screen | Sound |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1–2 | 0:00–0:04 | **Hook** | Paper card. Giant serif "Which country?" Clue stickers slam in on beats 2, 4, 6: *LANDLOCKED · DRIVES ON THE LEFT · 8 NEIGHBOURS*, then "Answer at the end" countdown | Tick, then a plucky riff intro |
| 3–5 | 0:04–0:10 | **Scalar** | Venn wipe in. Guesses typed fast. Cell cascades flip green/orange/amber. "↑ Go higher" pops. Punch-in on the arrow. Ends on a suspenseful cut before the answer | Flip whooshes on beats; build starts |
| 6–8 | 0:10–0:16 | **Continuum** | Venn wipe in (pink). Three country cards. Drag one into the ranking: ding. Drag one wrong: shake, a heart cracks | Ding, shake sting |
| 9–11 | 0:16–0:22 | **Whenabouts** | Venn wipe in (yellow). Year scrubber whip-pans forward and back, through a BCE year, and locks. "Exact Year" stamp | Rewind whoosh, stamp thud; build peaks |
| 12–15 | 0:22–0:30 | **Tectonic** | Power-off cut to dark **on the downbeat of bar 12**. Globe assembles; countries snap in on off-beats; counter 1→196 accelerates | **Drop.** Snap notes |
| 16–17 | 0:30–0:34 | **Payoff** | Four game cards slam into the 2×2 Today lineup. "4 of 4" stamp. Riddle answer **ZAMBIA** stamps with confetti | Big hit, then a beat of quiet |
| 18–19 | 0:34–0:38 | **CTA + credit** | "4 daily puzzles. Free. No sign-up." Logo, scalargame.com, "Play one. It takes 3 minutes." Small line: "Built by Samir" | Riff resolves; loops to 0:00 |

**Riddle answer:** **Zambia** (verified unique: landlocked + drives on the left + 8 land neighbours). Note "drives on the left" is a hidden data field, not a Scalar UI clue, so the video presents these as riddle clues rather than as Scalar's own clue cards.

**Copy rules:** short, dry, confident. Never "revolutionary" or "addictive." No comparison claims about other games.

## Teasers (13–15s each, 7 bars)

Common shape: **hook (0–2s) → the mechanic (2–11s) → payoff (11–13s) → CTA (13–15s)**. The CTA is "Play today's puzzle" plus the game's URL.

- **Scalar:** hook "6 clues. Name the country." Three clue cards, a countdown, guesses flip through thermal colors, answer stamps. Teal accent.
- **Continuum:** hook "Rank these by population. No googling." Drag, ding, wrong-drop shake, three hearts. Pink accent.
- **Whenabouts:** hook "What year was this?" An event card, scrubber whip, grade stamp ("Right Era"), score pop. Yellow accent. Uses the real event images (owner accepted the licensing risk).
- **Tectonic:** hook "Build the world." This teaser is a cut-down of the ASMR piece with the CTA end card. Orange accent.

## Play-along series (15s template, data-driven)

One Remotion composition per game, fed from real data. New episodes need only a data row, not new animation.

**Template on the beat grid (7.5 bars):**
1. **0:00–0:02** Hook: "Pause & guess." Question slams in.
2. **0:02–0:04** Count-in: 4 beats, tick.
3. **0:04–0:10** Think window: a timer ring drains over 12 beats while the music tension builds. Viewers guess in the comments.
4. **0:10–0:13** Reveal on the drop: answer stamp, thermal flash, confetti if it was hard.
5. **0:13–0:15** CTA: "Today's set is live. Link in bio."

**Series and launch episodes (2 per game):**

| Series | Question | Source |
|---|---|---|
| **Guess the Country** | Three real clues from the Scalar data | `gameData.json` |
| **Higher or Lower?** | "Which has more people?" / area / GDP per capita | Continuum metrics |
| **What Year?** | One real Whenabouts event | published puzzle shards |
| **Where Does It Go?** | A country is highlighted on the globe; viewer names it | Tectonic `countries.json` |

**Hard rule:** episodes use only **ended** puzzle dates or curated non-live content, never today's live answer. Use the same UTC−12 "ended date" rule as the answer archive routes. Each episode's clue set is verified to have exactly one answer.

## Tectonic ASMR (20–25s, no voiceover)

- **"Build the world" (globe):** the seed country glows and hums. Each placement snaps in with a *thock* and a pentatonic note. The camera frames the growing cluster. A continent completes with a chime. A time-lapse fills the globe. Last frame: "196 countries." It dissolves back to the empty globe so it loops.
- **"Blast & reassemble" (Regions):** a level starts assembled, scatters in a tectonic blast, then pieces snap back in on the beat. Caption: "200 levels."
- No music track. Captions stay minimal.

## Production approach

This is a plan only; nothing is built yet. I recommend a hybrid in a **Remotion** project (React, matches the stack, renders 1080×1920 from code):

- **Scalar, Continuum, Whenabouts:** rebuild the UI moments in Remotion using the real components and real data where practical. That gives crisp, frame-accurate, fully controllable motion. ## Publishing and measurement

- **Cadence (suggested):** launch day hero, then a teaser a day for four days, play-alongs 3–4 per week, ASMR weekly.
- **Links:**
  - Instagram Reels captions aren't clickable. Use the bio link, Story link stickers for per-game links, and a "comment PLAY for the link" reply. Verify current Instagram link rules before launch.
  - YouTube Shorts: put the deep link in the description and a pinned comment.
- **Tracking:** `acquisition.ts` already captures `utm_source`, `utm_medium`, `utm_campaign` and `utm_content`. Proposed naming: `utm_source=instagram|youtube`, `utm_medium=reel|short`, `utm_campaign=launch_hero|teaser_scalar|playalong_ep03|asmr_globe`. **Verify** these values survive the `safeValue` sanitizer with a test link before launch.
- **Funnel (PostHog):** landing/direct entry → game loaded → first meaningful action → terminal Daily result. Report denominators and sample counts.
- **Video metrics:** 3-second hold rate, average view duration, completion/loop rate, saves and shares. **Set numeric targets after the first batch;** we have no baseline yet, so any target now would be invented.

## Risks and licensing

- **Music license:** check each downloaded track's license (some require attribution, which goes in the post description) and test-upload privately to see whether a platform flags it.
- **Whenabouts imagery:** accepted risk (owner decision, 2026-09-28). Credit sources in descriptions where required.
- **Natural Earth and map data:** Natural Earth is public domain, and the site already credits it. Keep the credit in any video that shows a map.
- **Honesty:** no fabricated stats or testimonials. Every number comes from the data or a recorded run.
- **Spoilers:** never show a live answer. Only ended puzzles, verified unique.
- **Platform rules:** verify current Reels and Shorts length limits, link handling and audio rules before publishing.
