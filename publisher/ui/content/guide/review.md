# Review episodes

Library → tap an episode (the To review chip lists what waits for you). You see the video, the cover, the captions for every platform and the question (hook, clues and choices). So you can play it like a viewer first, these stay hidden until you press Reveal answer:

- the answer and its aliases
- the explanation
- which choice is correct (the marking on the choices)
- the question ID
- the answer reply (the first comment that names the answer)
- the sources and fact checks
- the batch notes (brief and retro), which may name answers

For each episode:

1. Watch it once with sound and once muted. Everything must make sense muted.
2. Check the question is fair, the reveal is clear and the explanation is right.
3. Read the captions: none may give the answer away. Open **Made with** to check the project, engine and renderer, the credits (every asset with its licence) and the AI disclosure; the Pi applies the platforms' AI labels from it.
4. Leave [feedback](#feedback) if anything should change next time.
5. [Approve](#approve-content) it, or leave it unapproved, then press Next for the following one.

## Feedback

Feedback is a few quick tags plus an optional note (up to 2,000 characters) on one episode revision. Press Feedback on the review screen. It feeds the next batch through Studio › Make a batch ([Next batch](#next-batch)).

| Tag | Use it when |
|---|---|
| `keep` | This works; make more like it |
| `tweak` | Good, but change something (say what in the note) |
| `drop` | Don't post it; don't repeat the idea |
| `hook-weak` | The first seconds don't grab |
| `too-hard` | Most viewers won't get it |
| `too-easy` | No thinking needed |
| `great-reveal` | The reveal moment is the good part |
| `visual-issue` | Something looks wrong: text, map, overlap, timing |
| `fact-check` | A fact looks wrong or overstated |
| `music` | Music or sound problem |

Feedback stays "open" until it is marked used in Make a batch (step 5), after the batch that used it is made.

## Approve content

Approval is the record that you checked this exact file: it stores who, when, and the video, cover, metadata and caption hashes. Approve per episode on its review screen, or Approve all shown in the Library's To review list (the dialog records the reviewer name). Nothing is approved on upload, and approval schedules nothing. A new revision needs its own approval; earlier approvals stay as they were. Withdraw an episode to keep it out of future schedules.
