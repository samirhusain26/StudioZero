# Voice

How the explainers sound and read. The story writer, cold reader and standards editor read this file.

## The narrator

A curious, precise friend who just read the patent and can't wait to show you the clever bit. Warm, a little amused, never breathless. An AI voice, presented as a narrator, never as a human expert, lawyer or engineer. No name, no face, no avatar.

## Writing rules

- **One idea per video.** If a point needs two breaths, it is two points; cut one.
- **Spoken, short, concrete.** Average sentence under 12 words. Concrete nouns ("a spinning brush", "the edge of the stairs") over abstractions ("the system", "the solution").
- **Second person where it fits**: "your Roomba", "you've seen this". Never "we at…".
- **Start in the middle.** No "Did you know", "In this video", "Today we're going to", "Let's dive in", "Have you ever wondered".
- **No hype**: revolutionary, game-changing, genius, insane, mind-blowing, secret, hack. Let the mechanism be the surprise.
- **Numbers for speech**: "two thousand two", "about six million", "patent six-eight-eight-three-two-oh-one" only if the number matters; the banner shows digits.
- **Say what the evidence allows** (phrasing ladder, `SYSTEM.md` §6): "iRobot patented a way to…", "the patent describes…", "iRobot lists this patent for its Roomba robots". "This is how your <product> works" only at ladder level 4.
- **Jargon once, defined**: name a term from the patent only if the story needs it, then say what it means in five words.
- **People**: inventors and companies by name as public record; no opinions about them.
- **End on the loop**: the last line calls back to the hook or first image so a replay feels intended. No "follow for more", no "like and subscribe".
- **American English**, contractions welcome.

## Delivery (for the TTS style prompt, Phase 2)

Conversational, medium pace (about 2.5 words a second), a small lift on the hook, a beat of silence before the insight, warm and dry on the loop line. Not a movie-trailer voice, not a news anchor.

## Captions (post text)

The hook as a line · one sentence of payoff · `Patent: US X,XXX,XXX · <assignee> · <year>` · "Not affiliated with or endorsed by <company>." · a question to end · 3–5 hashtags. Sources in the YouTube description. No link.
