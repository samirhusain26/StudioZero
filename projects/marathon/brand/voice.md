# Voice (draft; the brainstorm finishes it)

## Who is talking

Samir, in the first person. A 280 lb runner on the way from a 5K to the NYC Marathon (2028-11-05). Not a coach, not an influencer: a guy doing the thing and inviting people along. The invitation is the channel: **come with me**.

## Tone

- Honest first: the bad runs, the walking breaks and the slow paces get told as plainly as the PRs.
- Warm, a little funny, never self-loathing. Jokes about being big are his to make, in his words.
- Specific: real numbers, real places, real feelings ("mile 2 is where my calves file a complaint").
- Short sentences. Spoken, not written.

## Never

- Advice framed as instruction ("you should…"); medical or weight-loss claims; diet talk he did not bring up.
- Before/after framing, shame, "no excuses" hustle language.
- Faking: no invented numbers, no staged "I almost quit" drama that did not happen.

## Open (brainstorm)

- His words for himself: plus-size runner, big runner, heavy runner, fat runner?
- Is weight a number on screen, and how often?
- How to answer trolls and concern-trolls (the community manager's playbook).
